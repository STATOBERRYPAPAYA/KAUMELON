Quick revision · 1

  • ANOVA compares population means by partitioning the total variation.
  • The one-way null hypothesis is \(H_0:\mu_1=\cdots=\mu_v\).
  • A mean square is a sum of squares divided by its degrees of freedom.
  • The treatment F-ratio compares treatment mean square with error mean square.
  • The usual exact F test assumes independent normal errors with a common variance under the specified model.

Quick revision · 2

  • A significant overall F indicates that at least one pair of means differs; it does not identify the pairs.
  • The additive two-way model separates treatment and block variation.
  • With one observation per combination, interaction cannot be estimated separately from error.
  • Check the design and residuals before interpreting the F tests.

Important formulae

For a one-way classification with \(v\) treatments and \(n\) observations, let \(T_i\) be the total and \(n_i\) the number of observations in treatment \(i\), and \(G\) the grand total.

\[CF=\frac{G^2}{n},\qquad SS_{Total}=\sum_{i,j}y_{ij}^2-CF\]

\[SS_{Treatment}=\sum_{i=1}^{v}\frac{T_i^2}{n_i}-CF,\qquad SS_E=SS_{Total}-SS_{Treatment}\]

\[MS_{Treatment}=\frac{SS_{Treatment}}{v-1},\qquad MSE=\frac{SS_E}{n-v},\qquad F=\frac{MS_{Treatment}}{MSE}\]

For a balanced additive two-way classification with \(v\) treatments and \(r\) blocks, one observation per combination, and block totals \(B_j\):

\[SS_{Blocks}=\sum_{j=1}^{r}\frac{B_j^2}{v}-CF\]

\[SS_E=SS_{Total}-SS_{Treatment}-SS_{Blocks}, \qquad edf=(v-1)(r-1)\]

Self-check from the chapter

  1. ANOVA stands for Analysis of __________.

    Variance

  2. The null hypothesis in a one-way fixed-effects ANOVA states that all population means are __________.

    Equal

  3. A sum of squares divided by its degrees of freedom is called a mean __________.

    Square

  4. The denominator of the usual treatment F-ratio is the __________ mean square.

    Error
