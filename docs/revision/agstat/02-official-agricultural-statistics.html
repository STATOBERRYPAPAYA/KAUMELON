Quick revision · 1

  • Crop production combines an area estimate and a corresponding yield estimate.
  • CCE plots must be selected by the prescribed sampling procedure, not by convenience.
  • Plot yield requires the net produce weight and the actual harvested area.
  • Villages, fields and experimental plots can form successive sampling stages.
  • Official estimates should be quoted with their reference year and release status.

Quick revision · 2

  • The Agricultural Census describes operational holdings.

Self-check from the chapter

  1. Crop production is estimated by multiplying crop area by average crop __________.

    Yield

  2. The field procedure used to estimate crop yield is called a crop __________ experiment.

    Cutting

  3. The full form of CCE is __________.

    Crop Cutting Experiment

  4. The full form of GCES is __________.

    General Crop Estimation Survey
