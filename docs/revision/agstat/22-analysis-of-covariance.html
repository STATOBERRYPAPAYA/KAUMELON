Quick revision · 1

  • ANCOVA combines treatment comparison with regression adjustment.
  • A covariate should be relevant to the response and should not be caused by the treatment being compared.
  • The common-slope model assumes the same regression slope across treatments; examine treatment-by-covariate interaction.
  • Independent errors, appropriate linearity and common residual variance are required for the stated model; normal errors support the usual exact tests.
  • Adjustment compares treatment means at a common covariate value.

Quick revision · 2

  • Estimating one covariate slope uses one additional error degree of freedom.
  • A useful covariate can improve precision, but improvement is not guaranteed.
  • Adjustment does not turn an observational comparison into a randomized experiment.
  • Use pair-specific standard errors when covariate means differ.

Important formulae · 1

For the CRD with one covariate and a common within-treatment slope:

\[Y_{ij}=\mu+\tau_i+\beta(X_{ij}-\bar X)+e_{ij}, \qquad \hat\beta=\frac{E_{xy}}{E_{xx}}\]

Here \(E_{xx}\), \(E_{xy}\) and \(E_{yy}\) are the within-treatment corrected sums of squares and products defined in the chapter, with \(E_{xx}>0\).

\[E=E_{yy}-\frac{E_{xy}^2}{E_{xx}},\qquad MSE_{adj}=\frac{E}{n-v-1}\]

With \(n\) observed responses, \(v\) treatments and one fitted covariate slope, the CRD error degrees of freedom are \(n-v-1\). For the complete RBD with \(r\) blocks, use the within-block-and-treatment quantities and error degrees of freedom \((r-1)(v-1)-1\).

\[\bar Y_{i,adj}=\bar Y_i-\hat\beta(\bar X_i-\bar X)\]

\[F_{Treatment}=\frac{(E_1-E)/(v-1)}{MSE_{adj}}\]

Important formulae · 2

Here \(E_1\) is the residual sum of squares from the reduced model without treatment effects, retaining the covariate and, in RBD, the blocks. \(E\) is from the full model. The treatment comparison is therefore adjusted for the covariate.

Self-check from the chapter

  1. ANCOVA combines analysis of variance with __________.

    Regression

  2. A measured explanatory variable used for statistical adjustment is called a __________.

    Covariate

  3. The common-slope ANCOVA model assumes equal regression __________ across treatments.

    Slopes

  4. A covariate measured before treatment application is called a __________ covariate.

    Pre-treatment
