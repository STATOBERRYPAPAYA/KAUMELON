1Who collects what

  • Agriculture is a state subject, but agricultural statistics are on the concurrent list, so the system is decentralised.
  • State Agricultural Statistics Authorities (SASAs) collect and compile at state level.
  • Nationally, the ES&E Division (Economics, Statistics and Evaluation) of the Dept. of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare compiles crop production estimates.

3Crop Cutting Experiment (CCE): steps

A small, randomly located plot is harvested and weighed to estimate yield. Yield surveys for principal crops run under the General Crop Estimation Survey (GCES).

  1. Select the field: it must be larger than the CCE plot.
  2. Locate the plot from the south-west corner using random steps along the horizontal and vertical sides.
  3. Mark and harvest plants rooted inside the pegged, string-marked plot; apply the boundary rule consistently.
  4. Thresh on a clean mat and dry the grain.
  5. Winnow and weigh: subtract the empty bag to get the net grain weight.

4CCE numbers

Check a square plot$$d = a\sqrt{2};\ 5\text{ m plot} \Rightarrow 7.07\text{ m}$$
Plot yield (kg/ha)$$\frac{10\,000\,w}{a}$$

$w$ = net grain (kg), $a$ = plot area (m²). 12.5 kg from 25 m² = 5000 kg/ha.

Plot shapes: 5×5 m, 10×10 m squares, 10×5 m rectangle; triangles (UP) and circles (WB) also documented.

7Agricultural Census

What

Structure of agriculture: number and area of operational holdings, land use, cropping pattern, inputs, down to village level.

When

Started 1970–71; quinquennial (every 5 years). 11th census: reference year 2021–22, launched 28 July 2022.

How

Land-record states: number and area by complete enumeration; characteristics on a sample basis.

Reference period

The agricultural year, July to June.

!Common mistakes

  • Kerala uses sample surveys for area, not complete enumeration.
  • Yield = production ÷ area, never ÷ number of farmers.
  • Don't mix up the current three advance estimates with older four-forecast schedules.
