Analysis of Variance

Partitioning total variation, degrees of freedom, mean squares, the F test, assumptions and ANOVA models.
5-minute revision

1The idea

ANOVA (Analysis of Variance) compares population means by partitioning the total variation into parts due to known sources and to error.

Null hypothesis (one-way)$$H_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2 = \cdots = \mu_v$$

A significant $F$ means at least one pair of means differs. It is an omnibus test and doesn't say which pairs.

2Assumptions

  • Observations from normal populations
  • Common variance: homogeneity of variance
  • Samples drawn independently
  • Treatment effects are additive

So errors are iid $N(0, \sigma^2)$. Check with residual plots: residuals vs fitted values, and a normal Q-Q plot.

3One-way ANOVA (CRD)

Correction factor$$CF = \frac{G^2}{n}$$
Total SS$$SS_T = \sum y_{ij}^2 - CF$$
Treatment SS$$SS_{Tr} = \sum \frac{T_i^2}{n_i} - CF$$
Error SS$$SS_E = SS_T - SS_{Tr}$$
Mean squares$$MS = \frac{SS}{df}$$
F ratio$$F = \frac{MS_{Tr}}{MSE}$$

$v$ treatments, $n$ observations, $T_i$ treatment totals, $G$ grand total.

5Two-way ANOVA (RBD)

Block SS ($B_j$ block totals)$$SS_B = \sum \frac{B_j^2}{v} - CF$$
Error SS$$SS_E = SS_T - SS_{Tr} - SS_B$$

Separates treatment and block variation. With one observation per cell, interaction cannot be separated from error.

6ANOVA models

Linear model$$Y_{ij} = \mu + \tau_i + e_{ij},\quad e_{ij} \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$$
Fixed$\tau_i$ unknown constants (specific varieties of interest)
Random$\tau_i \sim N(0, \sigma^2_\tau)$; variance components (randomly chosen fields)
MixedBoth fixed and random effects

Reject $H_0$ if calculated $F$ > table $F$ at ($v-1$, error df). Then use multiple comparisons to find which means differ.

!Common mistakes

  • Concluding all means differ from a significant $F$.
  • Forgetting to subtract the correction factor.
  • Deleting outliers just to change significance.

?Fill in the blanks

  1. A CRD with 5 treatments and 4 replications has error df.15
  2. An RBD with 5 treatments in 4 blocks has error df.12
  3. MS(treatment) = 40 and MSE = 8 give F = .5
  4. A grand total of 120 from 12 observations gives a correction factor of .1200
  5. A treatment SS of 60 with 4 treatments gives a treatment mean square of .20
  6. A significant F shows that at least pair of means differs.one
